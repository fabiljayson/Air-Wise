About GoTravel

We're on a mission to make flight booking smarter, cheaper, and stress-free.

Our Story

Founded in 2025, GoTravel was born from a simple frustration: flight prices change constantly, and travelers always wonder if they're paying too much.

Our team of engineers and travel enthusiasts built an AI-powered platform that analyzes millions of data points — seasonal trends, weather patterns, demand shifts, and historical pricing — to predict the optimal time to book your flight.

Today, we help thousands of travelers across Africa and beyond find the best deals, saving them time and money on every trip.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Innovation First

Customer Focus

Trust & Security
